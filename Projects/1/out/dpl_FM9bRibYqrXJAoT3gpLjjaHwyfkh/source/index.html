What are we?

We are a local chapter of Hack Club, with the primary goal of bringing our love of coding to the average teen, regardless of experience.





Here at Hack Club, we define hackers as people who 'hack' away at complex problems, like a wood-cutter, and find creative ways to cut it down and create wonder.
We hope that hacker can be you.

02 / PURPOSE

Why
are we?

I believe that coding is something that is exceptionally fun, alone or with friends... and Hack Club has revolutionised that for me.
'Hacking' has transitioned from a hobby to something I could not live without. I have made so many amazing friends, and lived as much of a goof as I want. Hack Club is the most accepting community I have ever seen, and I want to personally spread that acceptance across the word.
Starting with Rutlish High School.
